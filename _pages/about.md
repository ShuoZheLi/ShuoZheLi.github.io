Hi! I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by
[Prof. Amy Zhang](https://www.ece.utexas.edu/people/faculty/amy-zhang) and [Prof. Leqi Liu](https://leqiliu.github.io/)
supported by Open Philanthropy and the Wesley W. Calhoun, Jr. Endowed Scholarship.

I work on **reinforcement learning for large language models**: making LLM reasoning _capable_, _reliable_, and _deployable_.

<div class="research-thrusts">
  <div class="thrust">
    <div class="thrust-label">Capability</div>
    <div class="thrust-q">How can we help models learn to solve harder problems (<a href="{{ '/publications/#zhou2025expo' | relative_url }}">ExPO</a>) and recover from misleading reasoning (<a href="{{ '/publications/#li2026gasp' | relative_url }}">GASP</a>)?</div>
  </div>
  <div class="thrust">
    <div class="thrust-label">Reliability</div>
    <div class="thrust-q">How can we evaluate reasoning beyond final-answer accuracy (<a href="{{ '/publications/#pathak2026frs' | relative_url }}">FRS</a>) and improve reasoning without sacrificing calibration (<a href="{{ '/publications/#li2026carerft' | relative_url }}">CARE-RFT</a>)?</div>
  </div>
  <div class="thrust">
    <div class="thrust-label">Deployability</div>
    <div class="thrust-q">How can we adapt reasoning models to real-world constraints (<a href="{{ '/publications/#li2026dap' | relative_url }}">DAP</a>, <a href="{{ '/publications/#hu2025stitchllm' | relative_url }}">StitchLLM</a>)?</div>
  </div>
</div>

Before focusing on LLMs, I worked on offline RL ([Iterative Dual-RL]({{ '/publications/#xu2025idrl' | relative_url }})), Sim2Real
([Robot Air Hockey]({{ '/publications/#chuck2024airhockey' | relative_url }})), and navigation ([DynaBARN]({{ '/publications/#nair2022dynabarn' | relative_url }}))
in robot learning. I have also worked on financial time series forecasting
([WaveLSFormer]({{ '/publications/#li2026wavelsformer' | relative_url }}), [Stockformer]({{ '/publications/#li2025stockformer' | relative_url }})).
Before Ph.D., I worked on wireless sensing ([RFID]({{ '/publications/#pradhan2021rfid' | relative_url }})) and mobile systems ([VIGOR]({{ '/publications/#he2024vigor' | relative_url }})) with [Prof. Lili Qiu](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~lili/).
